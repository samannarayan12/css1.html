New Tech Startup Opens Research Hub

Back to News

A new technology startup has opened a research hub dedicated to artificial intelligence and sustainable product design. The facility will focus on developing solutions for clean energy, smart agriculture, and practical digital tools for everyday life.

Company leaders say the hub will bring together engineers, researchers, and local students to build innovative products with real-world impact. The initiative is expected to create new jobs and strengthen the region's growing technology ecosystem.
