City Launches Green Transport Initiative

Back to News

The city government has announced a major green transport initiative aimed at reducing emissions, easing road congestion, and encouraging cleaner daily travel. Officials say the plan will expand electric bus routes, improve bike-sharing access, and install more charging stations across major districts.

Residents are expected to benefit from reduced fuel costs and improved public transportation reliability. City planners also believe the initiative will support long-term sustainability and healthier urban living conditions for families, students, and commuters.
